Our Focus Is Helping Seniors Live Well At Home

De Luz Home Care LLC brings safe, loving, and caring services to those who desire nursing-home-quality treatment in the comfort of their own home. We help seniors and older adults with non-medical activities of daily living — including meal preparation, bathing assistance, dressing, light housekeeping, medication reminders, and transportation.

Care and companionship are available on an hourly or live-in basis. We are experts in 24-hour live-in care.
