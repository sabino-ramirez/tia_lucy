About Us

Our Focus Is Helping Seniors Live Well At Home

De Luz Home Care LLC brings safe, loving, and caring services to those who desire nursing-home-quality treatment in the comfort of their own home. We help seniors and older adults with non-medical activities of daily living — including personal care and grooming, meal preparation, medication reminders, light housekeeping, companionship, and help with shopping and errands.

Care and companionship are available on an hourly or live-in basis. We are experts in 24-hour live-in care, with specialized experience supporting families living with Alzheimer's, dementia, and Parkinson's.
